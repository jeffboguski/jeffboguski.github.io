Career Overview

My career in live entertainment began in Winnipeg in 1989. Starting from the technical side of production, I developed experience across touring, live events and production management, eventually taking on leadership roles with touring productions throughout North America and internationally.

1989–2000 — Technical Foundations & Touring

I began working in live production while studying electrical engineering at the University of Manitoba, supporting campus productions through the UMSU technical team.

After university, I continued working in live entertainment as a freelance technician and production professional, becoming a member of IATSE Local 63 in 1995.

During this period I worked in touring and production roles including work with The Watchmen, Lilith Fair and Iron Maiden.

2001–2015 — Stage Group International

As a Principal Partner and Senior Production Manager with Stage Group International, I worked with touring productions across North America and internationally.

My responsibilities included production management, budgeting, staffing, logistics, contracts, immigration, ticketing and show settlement.

2000s–Present — Night Castle Management

Through Night Castle Management, I oversee production for Trans-Siberian Orchestra, Savatage and American Young Voices.

The work involves managing the production requirements of large-scale touring projects and coordinating the many moving parts required to deliver them on the road.

Production Strategy

Through Production Strategy, I continue independent production management and consulting work within the live entertainment industry.
