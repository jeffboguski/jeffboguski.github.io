Career Overview

My career in live entertainment began in Winnipeg in 1989, working on the technical side of live production while studying at the University of Manitoba. Over time, I expanded into touring, stage & production management, technical design and budgeting. I had the good fortune early on to work with a diverse mix of North American and international clients.

1989–2000 — Technical Foundations & Touring

I began working in live production while studying electrical engineering at the University of Manitoba, supporting campus productions on the UMSU technical team.

After university, I continued working in live entertainment as a freelance technician and production professional, joining IATSE Local 63 in 1995.

During this period, I freelanced production roles at Westsun, Sound Art and independant clients. Noteable projects included Sunfest, Winnipeg Folk Festival, The Watchmen, Lilith Fair and Iron Maiden.

2001–2015 — Stage Group International

My next major project was a partnership in the production company Stage Group International based out of Toronto, Ontario. During my role as Senior Production Manager I planned and managed touring productions worldwide.

Responsibilities included production management, Technical design, budgeting, staffing, logistics, contracts and immigration.

2016-Present - Production Strategy

15 years on I moved to New York City and started the independent production management and consulting company Production Strategy Inc. Production Strategy was the natural move to fully implement all the relationships and skills I had built with my new base in New York City.

2023–Present — Night Castle Management

23 years of working with the Trans-Siberian Orchestra created a unique opportunity to work full time at Night Castle Management. I'm now 2 years into full time production oversight of their top 3 properties - Trans-Siberian Orchestra, Savatage and American Young Voices. My role includes production planning, budgeting, technical design and implementation, staffing, vendor coordination and touring operations.
