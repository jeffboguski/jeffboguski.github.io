Career Overview

My career in live entertainment began in Winnipeg in 1989, working on the technical side of live production while studying electrical engineering at the University of Manitoba. Over time, my work expanded into touring, production management and production leadership roles with productions across North America and internationally.

1989–2000 — Technical Foundations & Touring

I began working in live production while studying electrical engineering at the University of Manitoba, supporting campus productions on the UMSU technical team.

After university, I continued working in live entertainment as a freelance technician and production professional, joining IATSE Local 63 in 1995.

During this period, I worked in touring and production roles at Westsun, Sound Art and freelance work. Projects included Sunfest, Winnipeg Folk Festival, The Watchmen, Lilith Fair and Iron Maiden.

2001–2015 — Stage Group International

As a Principal Partner and Senior Production Manager with Stage Group International, I managed touring productions across North America and internationally.

Responsibilities included production management, Technical design, budgeting, staffing, logistics, contracts and immigration.

2000s–Present — Night Castle Management

Night Castle Management

At Night Castle I'm responsible for production oversight of Trans-Siberian Orchestra, Savatage and American Young Voices, including production planning, budgeting, technical design and implementation, staffing, vendor coordination and touring operations.
