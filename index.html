About

I have worked in live music and touring since 1989, beginning in Winnipeg and eventually working on productions across North America and internationally.

I started out on the technical side of live production and gradually moved into touring and production management, working across music, theatre and live entertainment.

Today I oversee production for Trans-Siberian Orchestra, Savatage and American Young Voices through Night Castle Management, while continuing independent production work through Production Strategy.

Career

My career began with technical work and touring in Winnipeg in 1989. I went on to freelance in live production and became a member of IATSE Local 63 in 1995.

From 2001 through 2015, I worked with Stage Group International as a Principal Partner and Senior Production Manager, leading production work for touring productions in North America and internationally.

My work has included productions involving Trans-Siberian Orchestra, KISS, Dream Theater, Cavalia, The Ten Tenors, Spirit of the Dance, NXNE and other touring productions.
