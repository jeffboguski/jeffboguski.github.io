About

I have worked in live music and touring since 1989, beginning in Winnipeg and eventually working on productions across North America and internationally.

My career has included technical work, touring, production management and production leadership across music, theatre and live entertainment.

Today I oversee production for Trans-Siberian Orchestra, Savatage and American Young Voices through Night Castle Management, while continuing independent production work through Production Strategy.

Career

My career began with technical work and touring in Winnipeg in 1989, followed by freelance production work and membership in IATSE Local 63.

From 2001 through 2015, I was a Principal Partner and Senior Production Manager with Stage Group International, leading production work for touring productions in North America and internationally.

My work has included productions involving KISS, Trans-Siberian Orchestra, Dream Theater, Cavalia, The Ten Tenors, Spirit of the Dance, NXNE and others.
